# Does Airbnb Work Outside Cities?

Airbnb is often associated with city apartments, weekend breaks and urban tourism.

But short-term rental is not limited to cities.

Travellers also use platforms such as Airbnb to find cabins, guest houses and small holiday homes in forests, countryside, mountains and coastal areas.

In fact, rural accommodation can offer something a city apartment cannot: **the location itself can be the experience.**

## Rural Accommodation Can Be the Destination

In a city, guests often choose accommodation because it is close to something.

Restaurants. Museums. Public transport. Attractions.

A rural guest may be looking for something completely different:

* Nature and outdoor activities
* Privacy and quiet
* A lake, coastline or mountain setting
* Hiking and cycling
* A private sauna
* Space for family or friends
* A romantic or relaxing getaway

A small cabin in the forest with a sauna and a view over a lake is not simply somewhere to sleep.

It can be the reason for the trip.

That changes the meaning of **location, location, location**.

The property does not necessarily need to be close to a major destination if the surroundings are themselves desirable.

## Is There Demand for Rural Airbnb?

The European short-term rental market is substantial.

According to Eurostat, guests spent **951.6 million nights** in short-term rental accommodation booked through online platforms across the EU in 2025, an increase of 11.4% from 2024.

Airbnb has also reported strong interest in non-urban destinations. In 2026, the company said that more than half of summer bookings in Germany and France were going to rural destinations.

These figures do not mean that every rural property will succeed.

They do, however, show that short-term rental demand extends well beyond major cities.

## What Makes a Rural Airbnb Attractive?

Simply being rural is not enough.

A successful property usually gives guests a clear reason to choose it.

That might be:

**Nature:** A forest, lake, coastline or mountain setting.

**Privacy:** A detached building with its own entrance and outdoor space.

**Amenities:** A sauna, hot tub, terrace, fireplace or other features that make the stay memorable.

**Design:** A well-designed small property can become part of the experience rather than simply a place to sleep.

**Activities:** Hiking, cycling, skiing, swimming, fishing or other things guests can do nearby.

The strongest rural properties often combine several of these.

## Seasonality Still Matters

Rural Airbnb does not eliminate seasonality.

A lake destination may be strongest during summer. A ski area may depend on winter. A countryside property might attract weekend visitors throughout the year but see much higher demand during holidays.

This makes it important to look beyond peak-season prices.

A property that works only for eight weeks of summer may have a very different business case from one that can attract guests throughout the year.

## Accessibility Matters Too

There is another side to rural location.

Guests may want to escape the city, but they still need to be able to reach the property.

Poor roads, limited public transport or a long distance from airports and major attractions can reduce demand.

This is why the best rural locations are not necessarily the most remote ones.

Being **far enough away to feel like an escape, but accessible enough to be convenient**, can be a valuable combination.

## A Guest House Can Be a Different Kind of Airbnb

A rural guest house does not have to compete with a city apartment.

It can offer something different: **space, privacy, nature and experience**.

This can make small detached guest houses particularly interesting. A compact property with a bedroom, bathroom, sauna and outdoor space may provide everything a couple or small family wants for a short escape.

For homeowners, there is another advantage: the guest house can remain available for family, friends or personal use when it is not being rented.

## So, Does Airbnb Work Outside Cities?

**Yes — but the better question is whether your location gives people a reason to visit.**

Rural short-term rentals can work when the property offers something guests actively want and the numbers work after considering seasonality, operating costs and local regulations.

The opportunity is not to copy what works in cities.

It is to offer something cities cannot.

Sometimes, the distance from the city is not the problem.

**It is the point.**

## FAQ

### Can an Airbnb work in a rural area?

Yes. Rural properties can attract guests looking for nature, privacy, outdoor activities and a break from urban life. Local demand and regulations still need to be assessed.

### What makes a rural Airbnb successful?

Location, the experience offered, pricing, accessibility, amenities and seasonality all matter. A strong property gives guests a clear reason to choose it.

### Are rural Airbnbs seasonal?

Many are. The degree of seasonality depends on the destination and what guests can do there throughout the year.

### Does a rural Airbnb need to be a large house?

No. A small, well-designed guest house or cabin can work if it offers privacy, desirable surroundings and features that make the stay worthwhile.
